Values with a rank

A ranking article can print each rate with its league rank after it, as in "47% (9)". The ranks follow the rates, so the number before the parenthesis is the cell's value and the extension reads it. A parenthesis that counts something else, such as volunteers of whom some are new, does not follow the value and stays text.
