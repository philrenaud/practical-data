Short box score totals

A football box score splits each team's rushing into a names table and a numbers table, and ends the numbers with an unlabeled team row. With only three runners, the team row sums carries, yards and touchdowns, but its average is the team's average and its longest run is the longest of the others. Neither adds up, and both sit inside the other rows' range. The team row must still be left out of the color scale. The short standings below end with an ordinary team, which stays on the scale.
