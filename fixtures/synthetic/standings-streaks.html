Standings streaks

A standings table ends with each team's current streak: "W6" or "L1" on a search results card, "1W" or "5L" on a league site. A streak is wins minus losses, so L5 is the worst and W6 the best. A letter after a number can also be a unit: a column of "5L" with no wins and no streak header is liters.
