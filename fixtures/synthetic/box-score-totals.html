Box score totals

A box score can put the player names in one table and the numbers in a second table beside it. The last row of the numbers is the team total, with no label in that table. Its numbers add up to the rows above, so the extension leaves it out of the color scale. The standings below end with an ordinary row, which stays on the scale.
