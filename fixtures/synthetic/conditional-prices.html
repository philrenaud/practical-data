Prices with a condition

A price list can split one product into two rows, each with a note under the price that says when it applies: "for requests up to 100,000 units" and "for requests over 100,000 units". The number before the note is the price, so the extension reads it. A bowling figure such as "3 for 45" is two numbers, not a price with a condition, and stays text.
