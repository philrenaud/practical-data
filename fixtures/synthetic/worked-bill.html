A worked bill

Documentation often works one bill through: a few line items, how each is calculated, its cost, and a Total row under them. The table has one number column, so the sums alone are not enough to call the last row a total, but its label says so and its cost is the sum of the others. The extension leaves it off the scale. The plan table below ends with a plan whose name starts with "Total" and whose price is not a sum, and it stays on the scale.
