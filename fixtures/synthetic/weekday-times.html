Weekday times

A league's weekly schedule can give each game a weekday and a kickoff time, as in "Sun 1:00PM", without a date. The extension reads these as times in a week and shades them from earliest to latest. The week starts after its longest gap, so a Thursday game comes before the Sunday games and the Monday game comes last.
