Temperature direction

Whether warmer is better depends on the season and the reader, so a forecast's temperatures lean only mildly toward "higher is better". Such a lean is not enough: the column reads neutral and paints cold to warm. A temperature with a clear better end, such as a cooler's load temperature, keeps its direction, and the forecast's other columns keep theirs.
